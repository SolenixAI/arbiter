# Glossary

The terms of this repo. Each term has one meaning and one source: the linked section of [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). This page names each term. It does not restate a rule, so it cannot drift from the file.

- **Arbitrary success**: the north star of the file. [Root](../AGENTS.md#arbiter)
- **Docus, Experis, Imples**: the three parts of each section: text, formula, diagram. They must agree. [Root](../AGENTS.md#arbiter)
- **`{[ ]}`, `{{ }}`, `:< >`**: parts that work together; a closed, complete set; generates. [Root](../AGENTS.md#arbiter)
- **5:0**: the goal state, with zero open fragments in scope. [§1](../AGENTS.md#1-goal-state)
- **AUX**: the agent-user experience, the two sides of every experience. [§2](../AGENTS.md#2-optimal-aux)
- **Fib3 tree**: the search tree for open questions on a hard problem. [§3](../AGENTS.md#3-learn-how-before-doing)
- **POE**: predict, observe, explain: how you prove that you understand a tool. [§4](../AGENTS.md#4-install-use-configure-understand)
- **SOT**: the single source of truth for a fact. [§5](../AGENTS.md#5-decide-from-data)
- **PVI²**: north star, inspiration, purpose, value, impact. [§6](../AGENTS.md#6-compounding-wins)
- **The ratchet**: each kept win becomes the next baseline. [§6](../AGENTS.md#6-compounding-wins)
- **TTT**: time to terminus, the time predictions to the goal. [§6](../AGENTS.md#6-compounding-wins), [§7](../AGENTS.md#7-time-matters)
- **One-way door**: an action that you cannot undo, or that other people can see. [§8](../AGENTS.md#8-do-the-work-three-pathways)
- **Fragment**: a piece that exists but is not resolved yet, in one of five types. [§12](../AGENTS.md#12-fragments)
- **Body**: a disjoint system that holds a fact. [§13](../AGENTS.md#13-the-three-body-problem)
