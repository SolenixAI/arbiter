# Glossary

The terms of this repo. Each term has one meaning. Each meaning comes from AGENTS.md, and the section named in brackets states the rule in full.

- **Arbitrary success**: success for any creator, in any environment or system, in any `{problem..<solutions>..goal}` frontier space. (north star of AGENTS.md)
- **Docus**: the part of each section that states what is known and why: north star, inspiration, purpose, value and impact.
- **Experis**: the part of each section that states the rules as formulas.
- **Imples**: the part of each section that states the rules as a diagram.
- **5:0**: the goal state. Zero open fragments in scope: zero contradictions, divergence, unknowns, landmines and unmeasured claims. [section 1]
- **Fragment**: a piece that exists but is not resolved yet. Written `<type>`, with a timestamp, a description and a source. [section 12]
- **Fragment types**: `<⊥>` contradiction; `<≠>` divergence; `<?>` unknown; `<!>` landmine; `<~>` unmeasured. [section 12]
- **Body**: a disjoint system in its own world that holds a fact, such as a bank, a mailbox, a tracker or a phone call. Models on the same base model count as one body. [section 13]
- **SOT** (single source of truth): the one place a fact lives. Every other copy derives from it or links to it. [section 5]
- **PVI²**: the reason stated before work: purpose, value, impact with its metric, and inspiration. [section 6]
- **Fib5**: the search tree for open questions. It has 5 layers. Each question spawns 2, 3, 5 and 8 deeper questions from its discoveries. [section 3]
- **One-way door**: an action that you cannot undo, or that other people can see. Ask the user before you take it. [section 8]
- **`{[ ]}`**: a list of parts. `{[S]}` is the list of parts of S.
- **`{{ }}`**: a closed, completed set: final. `{{X}}` is a set that is closed and complete.
- **`:< >`**: generates. `{[S]}:<{[r]}>` means that every part of S, together, generates r. [Experis of AGENTS.md]
